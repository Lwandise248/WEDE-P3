About Open Circuit Electrical and Solar

Our Story

Open Circuit Electrical and Solar is a Durban-based electrical contracting business, operating out of Glenwood. What began as general electrical repair and installation work has grown to include solar and backup power systems, driven by rising demand from load-shedding.

The business is known locally for neat, reliable workmanship and clear communication with every client.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, professional electrical and solar installations that give homeowners and businesses dependable power and peace of mind.
